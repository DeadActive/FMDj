# FMDj: checking the GLO menu

The menu and its lines are in `docs/lsdj/CONTROLS.md`.

1. **PLAY** starts LSDj (or DMGo). Expect the screen in DMG green, the picture in the middle with a light green
   border above and below it (no status bar, no black strip).

2. **GLO** opens the menu over the picture's lower half. **PLAY** a song first: expect it to keep playing while the
   menu is open, and the picture's upper half to keep moving.

3. On SCREEN, **RIGHT** (the SELECT knob or ARP) goes through POCKET, LIGHT, SGB, DARK. Expect the upper half of the
   picture in each one's colours at once.

4. **DOWN** (the ALGORITHM knob or SAVE) to STATUS BAR, **RIGHT**: ON. Expect `FPS 59.7 CPU nn%` on the left under
   the picture, `GAPS 0` on the right.

5. **GLO** closes the menu. Expect the whole picture in the chosen colours, nothing lost in the song.

6. Power off and on, **PLAY**: expect the same theme and the status bar as you left them.

7. FX, LFO, EDIT, ENV: expect them to do nothing now; their options are the menu's last four lines.
