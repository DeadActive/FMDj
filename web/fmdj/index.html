FMDj

LSDj, the Game Boy tracker, inside the M-VAVE FM-1.

Install with your ROM Song page Try the simulator

What it is

FMDj is firmware for the FM-1. It runs a Game Boy emulator, and the Game Boy runs LSDj. The keys, buttons and knobs are the Game Boy's buttons, and the FM-1's screen is the Game Boy's.

LSDj is not part of it. You bring your own LSDj ROM: the install page puts it into the firmware in your browser, and the firmware made with it is for your FM-1 alone.

What it does

Songs and kits over USB
The song page connects to the FM-1 by USB in Chrome. It lists LSDj's songs, downloads and uploads projects and songs (.lsdprj, .lsdsng), and backs up or restores the whole save (.sav). A project that brings its own kits gets a kit bank on the FM-1.
Saving to flash
Hold REC for a second and the save goes into the FM-1's flash. PLAY after power-on loads it. The save uses the part of the flash where Hortator keeps its user samples (USR1 to USR3); they are lost at the first save.
Sound timing and speed
The sound is made as LSDj writes it, not once per Game Boy frame, so notes land on their beat. LSDj's wait loop is skipped without changing anything it does, which leaves the FM-1 time to spare. The status line shows the speed (SPD), the load (LOAD) and any seconds with a dropout (GAPS).
The install page
It checks your ROM runs, types in a demo song, and installs the firmware over USB, in Chrome or Edge. Your ROM file is read by the page only: it is sent nowhere.
The simulator
The same firmware runs in a browser page, behind the FM-1's panel drawn in the Game Boy's colours, with your own ROM. Songs and saves are kept in that browser.

The keyboard keys are the simulator's. Editing is as in LSDj: hold A (the top F) and turn SELECT to change a value by one, ALGORITHM by an octave or 0x10.

Put it on your FM-1

  1. Open the install page in Chrome or Edge and choose your own LSDj ROM (.gb). The page checks that it runs.
  2. Connect the FM-1 by USB, directly to the computer, and press Install. Do not unplug it while writing.
  3. On the FM-1, PLAY starts LSDj and PLAY again plays the demo song.
  4. Songs and kits go in and out with the song page.

No FM-1 at hand? Try it in the simulator.

If an install fails and the FM-1 no longer starts, recovering it needs a Transporter: FM-1-transporter. The author accepts no responsibility if your FM-1 is damaged or misbehaves because of this custom firmware. Use it at your own risk.
