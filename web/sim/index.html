FMDj simulator

The FM-1's FMDj firmware, running in this page with your own LSDj or DMGo! ROM.

Loading…

The ROM is read by this page only and kept in this browser; it is sent nowhere. LSDj is by Johan Kotlinski, DMGo! by Audiowanderer (free from audiowanderer.com); neither is part of this page: you need your own copy.

Songs and saves

A loaded project goes into LSDj's song list and LSDj starts again; open it there: PROJECT, LOAD/SAVE SONG, LOAD. Its kits come with it. The song you are editing is in the list only after you save it in LSDj (PROJECT, LOAD/SAVE SONG, SAVE). A .sav is the whole memory: every song and the one being edited. Everything is kept in this browser, as REC held does.
