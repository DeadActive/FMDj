FMDj: songs

Chrome or Edge on a computer, the FM-1 on USB, LSDj started (PLAY). The song you are editing is not in the list until you save it in LSDj: PROJECT, LOAD/SAVE SONG, SAVE. After an upload LSDj starts again; load the song there: PROJECT, LOAD/SAVE SONG, LOAD.

The page saves to the FM-1's flash after every change it makes. What you edit in LSDj is kept when you hold REC for a second (the status line says SAVED), or with "Save now" here.

Use this page while LSDj is idle: not while it shows its own save or load in progress.
