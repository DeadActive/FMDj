LSDj on the FM-1

This page puts your own LSDj ROM into the FM-1's LSDj firmware and installs it over USB. It works in Chrome or Edge.

The firmware here holds nothing of LSDj. Your ROM file is read by this page only: it is not sent anywhere, and the firmware made with it is for your FM-1 alone.

1. Your LSDj ROM

2. Install

Connect the FM-1 by USB, directly to the computer. Do not unplug it while writing.

After the install, PLAY starts LSDj and PLAY again plays the demo song. Songs and kits go in and out with the song page.

If an install fails and the FM-1 no longer starts, recovering it needs a Transporter: FM-1-transporter. The author accepts no responsibility if your FM-1 is damaged or misbehaves because of this custom firmware. Use it at your own risk.
